# Medication data boundary

Keel may bundle generic, source-cited medication and supplement interaction rules.
Those rules describe product capability, not what any person takes.

A user's medication names, doses, schedule, notes, and adherence records are
private vault data. They must not appear in source-controlled examples, fixtures,
documentation, telemetry, or build output. Medication context never suppresses a
critical-value escalation and Keel does not direct prescription changes.
